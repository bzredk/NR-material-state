# Adversarial review and version log

## Version 0.1 — 2026-10-23

Decision: reject the broad history-to-latent-to-control novelty claim. Preserve a conditional investigation of when residual material state changes constrained robot decisions under common feedback. The required experimental sequence is spatial 2D, full 3D articulated manipulation, then physical robot work.

## Limits of this audit

- Seventeen source PDFs were archived; method-relevant pages were read, and four important pages were visually checked. Full appendices/proofs were not universally verified.
- One relevant soft-grasper chapter remained access-limited. Its abstract was not used as a substitute for detailed reading.
- Several closest sources are 2026 preprints; conference acceptance was not assumed.
- Audio evidence includes Chinese recaps and an incomplete direct teacher fragment. The proposed control architecture is our design, not a verbatim Nadia prescription.
- The website was read live and archived. This audit maps the project's role to that roadmap but does not edit or publish the website.
- Existing experiment results were inspected, not rerun. No new material measurements, robot simulations, or physical trials were performed.
- Simulator integration, material parameters, resources, and final sample sizes remain unresolved. Structural validation of the JSON checks completeness, not scientific correctness or successful experiments.

## Future revision policy

Change task limits, splits, primary metrics, and power only using calibration/pilot/validation evidence before final test access. Record the reason, old/new values, code/data hashes, and whether a prior test was viewed. After unblinding, a changed study is a new version with a fresh independent test set. Preserve negative outcomes rather than rewriting the claim around the best result.
