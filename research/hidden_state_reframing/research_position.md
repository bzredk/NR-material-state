# Research position: object hidden-state inference and representation

Date: 2026-10-25. Status: evidence-based reframing and proposed design; no new physical or simulation experiment. This page supersedes the *scope and implementation priorities* of the October 23 plan, while preserving its sources and negative results.

**Main question.** From a robot's action-observation history, what object-state information must be retained and recursively updated to predict the consequences of relevant future interactions, and when does that information change a useful action? Deformable material is the present physical carrier of hidden dynamics, not the general research topic.

**Target.** Prioritize B: a predictive object-state representation whose adequacy is tested for an explicit sensor set, action class, horizon, and population. Use A, interpretable internal variables, as the instrumented reference in the current two-mode system. Use C, a probability belief, when histories leave ambiguity or uncertainty matters for probing/defer decisions. These are different objectives, not three competing network names. Neither an RNN architecture nor a small latent dimension establishes Markov sufficiency.

**Existing evidence.** SRC-history dependence demonstrates observation aliasing and history-based response prediction in the synthetic two-mode contact model. Its full-history, correct-family classical estimator is more accurate than the learned estimator, and its 0.2 s observer nearly matches the long-history network. SRC-controller transfer supports limited reuse across future controllers, with confounded model comparisons. SRC-shared feedback finds no meaningful action benefit in its common-observer/controller setting. Code inspection also shows observer-induced convergence and task-window saturation; shared feedback is not a general rejection of hidden-state utility and contains no true-state action-reference batch. See [existing_evidence.md](existing_evidence.md).

**Contribution candidate.** A controlled account of when physically realizable but observation-aliased object states can be inferred, compressed, updated, reused across interventions, and exploited before feedback makes their distinction irrelevant. A new inference method is justified only by a demonstrated deficiency of strong existing methods under matched information and resources. No new algorithmic contribution is established yet.

**Scope.** The first carrier is recoverable fast/slow contact dynamics. The first spatial mechanism experiment is plane-strain contact with lateral observations. The first robot operation is single-tool, constrained recontact/press/release, conditional on equipment verification. A second task or carrier is a later test of transfer. Same-material geometry transfer is not cross-mechanism transfer.

**Key gaps.** Current models already contain a hand-specified physical state and evolution law. Recursive learned-state adequacy, residual value of older history, credible true-state decision headroom, calibrated sensing, spatial 2D physics, full articulated simulation, and real-material evidence remain unestablished. Public lab equipment plus the user's ability to request it establishes a resource pathway, not an installed, measured project interface.

**Thesis role.** This is a concrete bridge from physically grounded concepts to action-conditioned change and qualified reuse in the [research roadmap](https://thesis-spine.link/thesis-spine-rendered). The desired output is a scoped statement of what the robot knows well enough to predict or act, and where it should probe or defer. It does not complete causal discovery, neuro-symbolic inference, minimality theory, or universal safety certification.

**Immediate decision.** Run the small X01-X06 diagnostic chain in [minimal_experiment.md](minimal_experiment.md) before a large neural benchmark. If true-state access adds no decision value in the declared task/controller, revise the task or narrow the claim. If a classical observer already captures the useful information, retain it. If a learned recursive representation adds value, identify the information or inference failure it resolves before making a novelty claim.
